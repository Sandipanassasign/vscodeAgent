# ⚖️ Compliance & Audit Requirements (Banking Edition)

Checklist for ensuring the project meets global banking and data privacy standards.

## 🏛️ Banking Regulations
- [ ] **Basel III**: Ensure capital adequacy and liquidity risk reporting modules are validated.
- [ ] **SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley)**: Verify internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR).
- [ ] **AML/KYC**: Validate the "Sanctions Screening" and "Suspicious Activity Report" (SAR) triggers.
- [ ] **Open Banking**: Ensure PSD2/Open Banking API compliance for third-party access.

## 🔒 Security & Data Privacy
- [ ] **PCI-DSS**: Level 1 compliance validated for payment handling.
- [ ] **GDPR/CCPA**: Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is masked and "Right to Erasure" is functional.
- [ ] **Data Residency**: Verify that customer financial data is stored within regional geographic boundaries as required by law.

## 📈 Operational Audit
- [ ] **Business Continuity (BCP)**: Disaster Recovery (DR) switchover tested with < 15 min RTO.
- [ ] **Audit Trail**: Full 7-year retention policy for transaction archives verified.
- [ ] **Model Risk Management**: For AI/ML systems, ensure validation by the Model Risk team.
